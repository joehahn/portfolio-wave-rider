News retrieval dashboard — GKG + Wayback (upstream of the curator)

Judges the news gathering, not portfolio gains: completeness of the historical pull and whether the calendar has gaps, upstream of the curator and free of PageRank-mooning. The pool the curator actually receives is the ranked top-100 (salience + authority + per-wave weighting) — the shape that resembles live WebSearch results, not a raw dump. Plot 8 tracks the Wayback join-rate — the share of each window's ranked articles that got an archived lede. 53 weekly 21-day windows.

Raw data (inspect any file): data/curator_runs/gkg-1yr-weekly-v2/ — per-window <date>-pool.json (ranked article list with a lede field = the Wayback join), plus _corpus/ (full gathering, one file per day). Example: 2025-05-04-pool.json.
