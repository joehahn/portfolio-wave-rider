News retrieval dashboard — GKG + Wayback (upstream of the curator)

Judges the news gathering, not portfolio gains: completeness of the historical pull and whether the calendar has gaps, upstream of the curator and free of PageRank-mooning. The pool the curator actually receives is the ranked top-100 (salience + authority + per-wave weighting) — the shape that resembles live WebSearch results, not a raw dump. Each article carries a lede: first a look-ahead-clean Wayback snapshot (≤ as_of); where Wayback has no capture, a live-fallback fetches today’s page (LOOK-AHEAD-BIASED, tagged separately). Plot 6 tracks both: the clean join-rate and the clean+live coverage. 30 weekly 21-day windows.

Raw data (inspect any file): data/curator_runs/gkg-2yr-weekly/ — per-window <date>-pool.json (ranked article list; lede = clean Wayback join, lede_live = biased live-fallback, lede_sources = the clean/live/none split), plus _corpus/ (full gathering, one file per day).
