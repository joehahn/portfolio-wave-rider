Health of the live WebSearch feed behind the Curator Forwardtest (CFT) — ingest quality, not market outcome. In chart 1 the bars count articles and the dotted line counts search hits (a different unit, right-hand axis): one story returned by three pulls in a day is 3 hits but 1 article. New to corpus is a strict subset of distinct articles seen — the gap between them is stories the feed already had. Bars flat at zero means the daily cron stopped. Non-articles are quote and ticker pages: stored, tagged, never fed to a curator. Sister page: RBS covers the bootstrap-era corpus.
