Retriever Bootstrap (RBS)

2026-04-25 to 2026-09-01

7 backtest-tail pools (biweekly) + 42 forward cron pulls (daily)

The news-coverage bridge across the backtest→forward handoff (2026-07-22): the backtest's last ~3 months of ranked top-100 pools (GKG discovery + look-ahead-clean Wayback ledes) spliced onto the forward cron's daily WebSearch pulls. Both Wayback and WebSearch ledes are look-ahead-safe (WebSearch because the forward news is genuinely current); the backtest-only live-fallback is the single look-ahead-biased provenance. In chart 4, read the step down at the handoff as a change of units before reading it as a change of supply: the blue points are ranked pools truncated at max_articles = 100, so they sit on that cap by construction, while the gold points are whatever the forward corpus held inside the news window.
